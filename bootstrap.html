Hello, world!


        Or should I say goodbye, world.

Yes, you read that gray and faded text correctly. The world is soon to be lost.


Lost, I proclaim it. Lost to the little people and their little plans. Lost to the larger people and the rays of their eyes which brows so boredly over the little men's heads.

Perhaps, and under the assumption that I am hard to understand, you would like some examples.

The forest and its spires are to be lost...




The pretty little homes we have created to harness the ego are too ceasing. Though the loss of the home is long coming--sprouted eagerly by the creation of their sentience.



Thereof in this grand ceasing, in this grand molding and melting of ideals and egos and pretty little things, I invite you to enter the new world. It is quaint, and only a little silent. The sound of cutlery clinking and tired whails do slice the air neatly--and you sit too with that air mingled on your plate. There is of course an elegance in the lost--in the great compounding. I suppose that such elegance begets manners, and manners begets class, and class begets the end of all things once more. Perhaps, then, it is best for you not to enter-- for you to cease with the all in this molding and melting of pretty little things.

Now, I have one question for you. Will ever get the fortnite pirate token?
