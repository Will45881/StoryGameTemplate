ON TWAIN.


And if verity takes hold of the heavens, stretched far above,

And if fate wrests me—Twain—from its determinism, then be it so.

Whereof my soul quivers, thereof my essence lies.

Be my end, Andrew. Be my collapse, forthwith!

Sorry for the spoilers, but I wanted your attention. Twain is a game developed by me and me alone. It is an exploration of grief, moving on, and frilly dresses.

You follow Andrew as he returns to his hometown with a "friend" named Ariane. Andrew feigns needing to get over his parents death, and Ariane would love to help. Both are lying. Together you argue, hide in closets, and eat eggs past 11. This game has great music, interesting visuals, and a story I'm truley proud of. It takes roughly an hour to complete.

Characters

Andrew:

Andrew is a spiteful young man living a dull cycle. He still paints, but he is terrified to show the world any of his art because it is proof he exists, that he is someone. The world kicks him once, and he begs it to do so again. He views himself as a sickness, one which will spread to anyone he cares for, rotting and destroying them until they too are will-less. He was not always this way. When he ran away to pursue painting, he also left his high school, a place where he was anxious but had many friends.

Ariane:

Ariane was Andrew's high school best friend. She had a crush on him for most of their time together. After Andrew ran away, her family further collapsed in the face of Ariane's depression. Her father's abuse grew, eventually killing Ariane's mother in an altercation when she was just 17. After this, she resolved that she'd grown up. The world was no longer a beautiful palace worthy of her poetry and awe. It was cyclical and self-destroying. She moved to the city and took up work as a waitress.

Eve:

Eve is a flashy and charismatic young girl who manipulates Andrew into fully realizing his hatred for his parents. She tells him that they have no love for him, and that he needs to leave with her to pursue his passions. She lived with an older sister who did not care for her. She projects her want for change onto Andrew out of fear that it's a burden she must face alone. Eve feels the need to be an adult woman despite her age, and acts accordingly. They flee to the city where she eventually abandons him.
