Sorry for the spoilers, but I wanted your attention.
Twain is a game developed by me and me alone. It is an exploration of grief, moving on, and frilly dresses.

You follow Andrew as he returns to his hometown with a "friend" named Ariane. Andrew feigns needing to get over his parents death, and Ariane would love to help. Both are lying. Together you argue, hide in closets, and eat eggs past 11. This game has great music, interesting visuals, and a story I'm truley proud of. It takes rougly an hour to complete.
